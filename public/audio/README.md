# Background music

The app loops **`public/audio/gallery.mp3`** quietly in the background.
Save your track with exactly that name, in this folder.

If the file is missing, the app generates its own soft classical-style music
in the browser instead, so the app always has music.

## The licensing rule (important!)

We play the music in a **public demo**, so it must be legally free to use:

- The **composition** of classical music is public domain (Bach, Chopin...
  died long ago). But most **recordings** are still copyrighted by the
  orchestra or label.
- So the **recording itself** must be:
  - **Public Domain** (Public Domain Mark) or **CC0**, or
  - **CC BY** (Creative Commons Attribution): allowed, but you **must credit
    it** in `CREDITS.md` (the app shows it under "Music credits").
- **Never** use tracks from Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music or commercial CDs.
- Avoid "NC" (non-commercial) and "ND" licences unless you've checked they fit.

## Where to find legal recordings

- **Musopen** – https://musopen.org/music/ : filter for *Public Domain* / *CC0*
  recordings.
- **Wikimedia Commons** – https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Audio_files_of_music :
  open each file's page and check the licence box.
- **Free Music Archive** – https://freemusicarchive.org : only use tracks
  marked **CC0** or **CC BY**.

## Calm pieces that fit a gallery

All of these compositions are public domain; look for a public-domain or
CC BY *recording*:

- Erik Satie – *Gymnopédie No. 1*
- J. S. Bach – *Air on the G String* (Orchestral Suite No. 3)
- Claude Debussy – *Clair de Lune*
- Frédéric Chopin – *Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2*

Tips: convert to MP3 if needed, keep it under ~5 MB, and pick a recording
that loops nicely. Then fill in the Music section of `CREDITS.md`.
