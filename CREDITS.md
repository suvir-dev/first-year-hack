# Credits

## Music

The background music file is `public/audio/gallery.mp3`. Fill this in when
you add it (required for CC BY recordings, good manners for the rest):

- Track title:
- Composer:
- Performer:
- Source URL:
- Licence: (Public Domain / CC0 / CC BY 4.0 …)

If `gallery.mp3` is missing, the app plays music it generates itself in the
browser (Web Audio API). That music is our own code: no licence needed.

The celebration chime is also generated in the browser.

## Narration

The history voiceover uses your browser's built-in speech (Web Speech API).

## Paintings

All paintings are public domain, from Wikimedia Commons. Sources are listed in
`public/paintings/README.md`.
